# HOB — player one-pager (trio Sealed)

*For a teammate who's new to Limited and isn't building the deck. The companion to [HOB.md](./HOB.md), which is the build reference and is far too long to hand anyone the night before.*

**Read time: 3 minutes.**

---

## The only thing you actually need to know

**There is no drafting. You don't build a deck. We build it for you, together, and you play it.**

We open 18 packs as a team, dump them in one pile, and make three decks out of them. Your job starts when the deck is finished and someone hands it to you.

That's it. The hard part of Limited — knowing what's good, reading a card pool, cutting the right stuff — is the part you're skipping.

---

## What the night looks like

1. Everyone opens their six packs and we photograph them (so we can split the cards back after)
2. We pile everything together and build three decks — about an hour, all three of us
3. You get handed a deck and play three or four rounds, best of three each
4. Rounds are ~50 minutes, casual, everyone's drinking and looking things up

**Prereleases are the most casual event in Magic.** People bring their kids. Nobody has played this set before either — the cards came out today. Everyone at your table is also reading cards for the first time.

---

## Your deck: Red/White Dwarves

One sentence: **play a creature every turn, attach weapons to it, attack.**

- You'll have ~16 creatures, a handful of Equipment, and 5–6 removal spells
- Equipment = artifacts you attach to a creature to make it bigger. You can move them between creatures for a cost
- "Storied" = once you control 3 artifacts/legends/sagas at any point, you permanently get a bonus. It happens on its own. Don't plan around it

This is the closest deck to your mono-green landfall deck: go first, curve out, hit them. You already know how to do this.

---

## Three habits to bring

**1. Don't spend removal on the first creature you see.** Your Constructed deck plays almost no removal, so this'll feel unnatural. In Limited it's the most valuable card in your hand. Hold it for something that actually threatens you.

**2. You're allowed to block.** Green landfall races. Limited games get decided on the ground and you'll often win by trading rather than by attacking.

**3. Play first, always.** When you win the die roll, choose to play. It's almost never wrong.

---

## Five things you'll see other people do

| Thing | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Amass Goblins** | They stack +1/+1 counters on ONE goblin Army token. It gets huge | Save one removal spell for it — killing it undoes their whole turn sequence |
| **Ferocious** | Bonuses when they control a creature with power 4+ | Kill the 4-power one and the rest of their deck turns off |
| **Recruit** | Draw a card, discard a card, make a 1/1 | Nothing to do, it's just value |
| **Adventure** | A card that's a spell first, then later a creature from exile | They get two cards out of one. Normal, not a trick |
| **Equipment** | Weapons that move between creatures | A small creature can suddenly be big. Don't assume a 1/1 stays a 1/1 |

---

## Two cards to actually watch for

- **Settle the Wreckage** — 4 mana, white, instant. Exiles *every creature you attacked with*. If your opponent has 4 lands untapped and two of them are Plains, don't attack with everything. Leave one or two home.
- **Dancing from Dark to Dawn** — green enchantment. If someone lands it, the game gets hard. Race them.

---

## When you don't know something

**Ask.** Ask your opponent what their card does — they'll tell you, it's expected. Ask us, we're right there. Nobody at a prerelease minds, and it's not cheating to read a card carefully.

If you're unsure whether to attack: ask yourself *"if they block and I lose the creature, am I still fine?"* If yes, attack.

---

## The actual bar

You need **2 of 3 team wins** per round, not 3. Your teammates cover the other two seats. You're not the load-bearing seat — you're playing the deck that's hardest to mess up, on purpose.
