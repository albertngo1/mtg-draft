## Format speed / meta read

**Unknown yet.** Every point here is a prediction from card text.

**From #879 (2026-11-10):**

- **Public reception is poor.** The hosts say the set "hit with a bit of a thud" (unpopular IP), so LR may not run its usual follow-up episodes. Expect few or no later TRK sources. Both hosts say the gameplay itself seems fine.
- **Strange New Worlds decks are slow** and win with card draw plus sweepers (Deep Space Scan, The Destruction of Praxis, Death Is Irrelevant). Their payoffs are mostly spells, so permanent-based "ramp" rares (Pah-Wraith, Stable Wormhole, Branching Timeline) don't fit them.
- **Federation counts add up fast.** An average deck has 3–5 creature types, which is why few cards carry the keyword.
- **Spacecraft tips:**
  - Station a little at a time so the ship isn't a creature during the opponent's turn.
  - Finish stationing on your own turn to dodge sorcery-speed removal (U.S.S. Discovery).
  - Hold back a full station when they leave removal mana up (U.S.S. Enterprise-A).
- **Beam Me Up resets finality counters** (Overflow Submatrix, Storied Veteran).
- **Dilemma:** 3–4 modal cards are enough for the rare payoffs (The Caretaker, Captain Benjamin Sisko). The rare choice lands count as dilemmas.
- **Splashing:** shocklands are C+ and slightly better because of Strange New Worlds. 5-mana single-pip bombs (Excalbian Gamemaster 5W, Borg Cube) are built to splash.
- **Grading lens this review:** favour "cards that just always work" and cheap must-kills (Subcommander T'Pol, Tasha Yar, Jadzia Dax) over flashy cards that sometimes do nothing.
- **Top rare/mythic calls:**
  - U.S.S. Enterprise-A A+
  - Q, Stacking the Deck A+ ("absolutely broken")
  - Mirror Universe Intrusion A ("maybe top five… might be the best card")
  - Armus, Skin of Evil A ("one of the best cards in the set")
  - Captain Jean-Luc Picard A, Excalbian Gamemaster A, The Wrath of Khan A, Captain Jonathan Archer A, Holo-Engineer A, U.S.S. Enterprise-D A


**From #878 (2026-11-04):**

- **Speed is the open question.** M expects (and hopes for) a slower format like Reality Fracture: 10 common tapped gain lands, spacecraft and the expensive synergy payoffs all want time. L: "if the format's fast, we're going to have to say goodbye to a lot of these interesting things."
- **Almost every card is a synergy card, but most serve two or three archetypes.** L calls the overlap deliberate and good: a black card that fails in one deck usually picks up slack in another. Off-plan cards with fine stats get marked down (Mugato, Shuttle Ace). Many "build-around" grades are really "play it in the deck that wants it" grades.
- **Mechanics.**
  - **Beam Me Up** — flashback-style recast from the graveyard, but you must also return a creature you control to hand. All are sorcery-speed so far. It turns into an upside only with cheap ETB creatures (Emergency Medical Hologram, Federation Probe, Assembly Specialist, Interstellar Effigy, Hoshi Sato). *(#879: underwhelming in play — see Supersessions.)*
  - **Face a Dilemma** — any modal "choose one" card triggers payoffs (Seven of Nine, Jett Reno, Tell Me What You See, Obsidian Order Operative, Gabriel Lorca, Captain Benjamin Sisko). Modal cards are seeded in every colour, but the payoff deck is UB.
  - **Federation** — counts creature types among your non-Borg creatures. Types are everywhere (most creatures have two), so L expects X to run about 1.5–2× your creature count. Saavik and Hoshi Sato are the payoffs graded B or better.
  - **Strange New Worlds** — counts *differently named* lands. Any two-colour deck starts at 2. Expect UG decks to run one off-colour basic of each type plus nonbasics. The cap is above 5.
  - **Spacecraft / station** (returning from Edge of Eternity) — most play as a mediocre sorcery that becomes a creature later. M is skeptical; L is "betting on spacecraft" because he counts enough artifact and spacecraft payoffs.
  - **Assimilate** (Borg) — put a card onto the battlefield as a Borg artifact creature with a +1/+1 counter. It's rare by design because every assimilate is costed as a free creature.
- **"Eight total power" (RW and a few others) plays like exalted:** build a board, then attack with one creature carrying all the bonuses (Una Chin-Riley, Cantankerous Captain, La'An). M is skeptical that you reach 8 power while the opponent still has removal.
- **Landcyclers** (one per colour at common: Free Borg Revolutionaries, Humpback Whales, Rogue Artificial Intelligence, Horta, Cold-Blooded Crew) — L predicted all of them at C+, coming off Reality Fracture where landcyclers were strong. They double as Strange New Worlds enablers.
- **Removal** — cheap red burn is plentiful and several commons/uncommons go face (Plasma Cascade, Transporter Malfunction, Khan's ETB). L flags a possible inflection point at 4 toughness because so much burn deals 3.
- **Lands are a real pick for UG.** The 10 common gain-land planets are C+. The five uncommon **Anomaly** lands (Artificial, Gaseous, Spatial, Subspace, Temporal Anomaly) are C+ to B− (L settled on B−). Each taps for colourless and sacrifices for an effect plus a New Planet token, so they are two different land names on their own.

## Archetypes

All ten pairs are two-colour, and each has **two signpost uncommons: one true gold and one hybrid.** No Sunset Show or data exists yet, so this is not a ranking. It is the WotC description plus the hosts' release-week lean, grouped as in the other LR guides.

**#879 rares that tilt the pairs:**
- **WB:** Borg Cube (BA A), Collation Trimatrix (BA A−), Overflow Submatrix (BA A−) and Primary Unimatrix Adjunct (near A with 12 artifact creatures) push WB artifacts up. WB is a "cheap artifacts reach critical mass" deck, not a ramp deck.
- **BR:** Gowron (very high), D-7 Battle Cruiser (BA B), General Chang (BA B) and Quark support Klingon sacrifice.
- **UG / Strange New Worlds:** The Destruction of Praxis (BA A), Deep Space Scan (BA B), Seven of Nine, Unassimilated (B+ / BA A−) and All Good Things . . . B+. It's slow, and wins on card draw plus sweepers.
- **RG ramp/landfall:** Will Riker (A− in landfall), Pah-Wraith (B in RG only), Genesis Device (BA B), Hit It! C+.
- **UB:** the dilemma deck gets The Caretaker B and Elim Garak B+. Mirror Universe Intrusion (A) is the best gold card.



**Hosts most positive (#878)**
- **WB Borg (artifact creatures).** It has the deepest strong cards: Locutus, the One Who Speaks B+, Resistance Is Futile B+, Zero, Incorporeal Sensate B, Malfunctioning Holodeck B, Rutherford B. Its build-arounds (Conquest Mission, Organic Avulsion Unit, Hive Mind Coprocessor) want 12–15 artifacts.
- **BG archaeology (graveyard; cards leaving the yard).** Vash B ("ask essentially nothing of you"), Daniels B, Hologram Stalker B+ ("Sun Titan and Grave Titan together"), Blastoneuron (B in BG). M's caveat: BG graveyard decks are often underpowered.
- **UG Strange New Worlds (ramp, many land names).** The hosts expect this to be the most interesting deck and to run 3–5 colours. Cloak and Phaser (BA B+) is the payoff, with Xenobotanist C+ and the Anomaly lands as enablers. The gold signposts are weak, though: The Traveler C+, D'Vana Tendi C.
- **BR Klingons (sacrifice / dies).** The Duras Sisters B, L'Rell B, Munitions Enthusiast B, D'Ghor Bloodletter B−, plus the cheap red burn.
- **UR scientists (noncreature spells).** Spock, Always Your Friend B+ and Anthropological Mission (BA B, a "win condition" enchantment). M strongly prefers the spell engines over the creature payoffs (Cetacean Ops D, Melkotian Adjudicator D), which "leverage your opponent's removal against you."

**Middle**
- **WU spacecraft / artificers.** Constable Odo B+ and Hemmer B. The spacecraft commons are only C to D+ on their own. L is betting the payoffs carry them; M is unconvinced.
- **UB dilemma control.** Seven of Nine (BA B) and Tell Me What You See (BA B) are the payoffs; Lore is only C. Its removal and card draw are good on their own.
- **GW doctors & counters.** The Doctor, EMH (BA B), Phlox C+, Sylvia Tilly B, Test of Conviction B−.

**Hosts skeptical**
- **RW eight-power "command".** Kirk, Commanding Officer B−, Worf, Honor-Bound Warrior D. M: "anytime I see eight power or greater, I'm like, really?"
- **RG away team (landfall + Beam Me Up).** Cleveland Booker D, Dal R'El C+. The good cards are Erica Ortegas (BA B), Exploratory Mission B− and USS Voyager (B in RG/UG).

## Card tips

Commons/uncommons are graded in #878; rares/mythics and the bonus sheet in #879, listed in a separate block per colour. Within each block, cards are ordered best to worst by grade.

### Multicolor (signpost uncommons, #878)

- **Constable Odo** — B+ (#878 release). 5-mana hybrid 4/3: stun-tap their creature (2 stun counters) or investigate twice. "Good at pretty much any point in the game." Fits WB artifacts too.
- **Locutus, the One Who Speaks** — B+ (#878). WB 2-drop: artifact creatures get +1/+1, and you can discard it as an instant to deal 2 to a creature and gain 2. Play it every time, even without artifacts.
- **Spock, Always Your Friend** — B+ (#878). UR 2/2; ETB look at 4 and take a noncreature, nonland card.
- **Hemmer, Fixing What Is Broken** — B (#878). WU 2/3 for 2 that gives spacecraft hexproof on your turn and loots when tapped.
- **The Duras Sisters** — B (#878). BR hybrid 2-mana 3/2 that dies into a 2/1 haste Klingon. "An easy two-for-one." Playable in any deck heavy in either colour.
- **L'Rell, Fierce Visionary** — B (M B/B−) (#878). BR 1/4 that makes a temporary 2/1 haste Klingon each combat; pay 2 and sac to surveil 1. Duras into L'Rell is a scary curve.
- **Zero, Incorporeal Sensate** — B (L started at B+) (#878). WB hybrid 2/2 lifelink: an Elite Spellbinder that taxes the exiled card by 2.
- **Vash, Freelance Archaeologist** — B (M "maybe B+") (#878). BG 2/4 for 3; upkeep surveil 1, and draws once per turn when a card leaves your graveyard.
- **Daniels, Temporal Agent** — B (#878). BG hybrid 4-mana 3/1 Gravedigger.
- **Seven of Nine** — BA B (#878). UB 2/4 for 4; draw a card whenever you face a dilemma. "There's going to be enough modal cards to make this work."
- **The Doctor, EMH** — BA B (#878). GW hybrid 5-mana 3/3 that puts a counter on a creature, then another on each creature with a counter. "The five-drop you'd want in green-white."
- **Kirk, Commanding Officer** — B− (#878). RW 3/3 for 3; exile-and-play the top card on an 8-power attack; sac for 2 to destroy an artifact or enchantment. Good only if RW is good.
- **Phlox, Unorthodox Doctor** — C+ (borderline build-around) (#878). GW 4-mana 3/4 trample that draws when another creature with a counter connects. Often clunky.
- **Dal R'El, Augment Orphan** — C+ (#878). RG 3/3 for 3; landfall adds R or G; pay 3 and sac a land to draw.
- **The Traveler** — C+ (#878). UG */3 reach vigilance; power = differently named lands, so it starts as a 2/3 for 2. It free-rolls on the other UG cards.
- **Lore, Positronic Provocateur** — C (#878). UB hybrid 3-mana 2/3 artifact: +1/+0 and unblockable, or surveil 2. "Trying to be all things to all decks." Better only if you care about modal cards.
- **Gray and Adira, Forever Joined** — C (BA C+ if generous) (#878). UR hybrid 4-mana 2/3 that copies a creature each combat if you cast a noncreature spell. "Too fragile… setup costs too high."
- **D'Vana Tendi, Optimistic Ensign** — C (#878). UG hybrid 2/4 that untaps a permanent (pseudo-ramp, double-station); pay 3 to surveil 1. Doesn't help Strange New Worlds.
- **Worf, Honor-Bound Warrior** — D / BA C (#878). RW hybrid 1/1 that doubles power on an 8-power attack and grows by tapping two untapped artifacts/creatures. "When it's not working, it's really not going to work."
- **Cleveland Booker, Empath** — D (#878). RG hybrid 4-mana 2/2 that makes a 2/2 can't-block Worm on landfall. Fails in the "behind" quadrant.
