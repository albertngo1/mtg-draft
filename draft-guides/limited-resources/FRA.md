## Format speed / meta read

**From #874 (strongest):**

- **Slow — the slowest format in quite some time.** You can landcycle on turn 2 and you're "not just dead". You'll be fine without a 2-drop, so don't put bad 2-drops in to raise the count. Counterspells are good because games go long and they answer expensive bombs.
- **Removal:** excellent from 2–4 mana, but there's **no cheap (1–2 mana) instant removal**, so sticky 1-drops like Stingerquill Voxmancer survive. A fast curve-out (e.g. Samut, Hazoret's Champion into 3-drops) is the thing this format is least set up to beat.
- **Balanced:** the hosts cite 17Lands with the bottom pair just over 53% and the top at 57%. Play counts are spread evenly, with no runaway deck. You can take the best card or the one that speaks to you: "pretty much all the ways are valid." Build-arounds largely work because the slow speed lets them "realize their equity."
- **Bombs are strong but beatable.** Ajani Unrelenting (?) was "pushing 70%" early. The removal and card draw are good enough that LSV won two Sealed boxes with Grixis decks that had few rares. **Sweepers are back:** Kindred Judgment, Overwrite the Multiverse, Austere Command (bonus sheet), Rise of the Deathbringer and Ajani Unrelenting's −3. Don't dump your hand.
- **Splashing gets "a little more of a green light than normal."** The bomb density, landcyclers and taplands make it work, and the landcyclers double as good 5–6-mana plays late. The hosts find many decks actually *improve* with a splash.
- **Landcyclers are all good**, and so are the dual lands (C+ held). "If the land cyclers are good, the format just can't be that bad."
- **Empower Jace plays great** and is full of micro-decisions. **Jace math:** with a Jace on 4, −3 to draw first and *then* empower again so you can −3 a second time. Piling loyalty onto one big Jace gives no extra activations. Firing the −3 whenever you can is the default. Jaces also act as life gain, because opponents divert attacks to them, and that is part of why the format is slow.
- **Decking happens.** Blue self-mill decks win with Fblthp, Impossibly Lost or Paradox Shaper. Be scared when a UB opponent is down to a few cards and still casting Omit Variables.
- **Aggro:** the hosts have seen almost none. Prowess isn't aggressive enough to matter.

**From #872 (release, still valid where #874 doesn't contradict):**

- **Main mechanic: Empower Jace N.** Put N loyalty counters on your Jace token, creating it first if you don't have one. The token has **−1: surveil 1** and **−3: draw a card**. Empower stacks. Empower 4+ cashes in for a card immediately; Empower 2 does not.
- **Every deck will have planeswalkers,** so "planeswalker matters" cards are real at common (dual lands, Tomik, Orzhov Lawmage, Mabel, Bitter Recluse, Archive Arbiter). Almost every removal spell in the set also hits planeswalkers.
- **Sequencing tip:** cast spells *before* combat. If the opponent answers with Countersculpt (which makes a Jace), you can still attack the new Jace.
- **Prepare.** Each allied pair shares one prepare spell: Peer Review WU, Omit Variables UB, Vicious Verse BR, Soul Tether RG, Seed Suture GW. **Threshold** (7+ cards in graveyard) lives in UB.
- ~~Speed: fast-ish~~ and ~~card draw overflows, cap it at 1–2~~ — **superseded by #874**, see above.
- **High-synergy set:** off-plan cards with fine stats get marked down. "This is not an artifact set": there are about 8 artifacts.

### Sealed (#874)

The hosts have played a lot of Arena Sealed (Win-a-Box). Draft is still the better format.

- **Usually 3 colours, sometimes 4, rarely 5.** A landcycler fixes only one colour once it's cycled.
- **No D-level cards, and very few C/C−.** Every card should remove something, be a threat that demands an answer, or advance mana or card advantage. A cheap body with no lasting impact "falls off hard."
- **Synergy cards that need other cards drop:** Unflinching Hortimancer ("just don't have this card in your deck"), Titanbones, Ghalta (?) (which Ghalta is unclear), Emergency Phytomedic (about 55% GIH in draft, but bad in Sealed) and Vigorbloom Vanguard. By contrast, Fatehold Chronologist has the same win rate in draft and Sealed, while Vanguard drops about 0.7% in Sealed. Divining Duelist is "kind of medium".
- **Aggro Sealed works only ~5–10% of the time** (one example was a low-curve UB deck with two Tetsuko Umezawa (?)). The hosts have leaned Grixis colours, but every colour is fine.

## Archetypes

All ten pairs are supported. Allied pairs are the five **Hexhaven** schools; enemy pairs are the five **planeswalker** decks. There's no Sunset Show yet.

**#874 ranking (early 17Lands, cited by the hosts; spread only ~53–57%, "a few tenths separate many of these"):**

1. **WU Fatehold — #1.** The synergy (scry/surveil payoffs) "you basically get for free": play good blue cards and supportive white cards and the triggers just happen. You don't need build-arounds like Eye of Jace. It also has strong upper-rarity cards.
2. **UB Theorix — #2.** Self-mill, card draw and removal. Paradox Shaper, Fblthp, Impossibly Lost and Countersculpt "are all good by themselves" in this shell.
3. **Everything else is close.** The hosts single out only the bottom: **WB is the worst pair so far.** The rest is qualitative: the +1/+1-counter cards (RW: Mabel, Valley Hero, Graft Surgeon) underperform, prowess (UR) isn't aggressive enough, and RG ramp works (mana matters, the top end pays off). The hosts have played Grixis and Grixis-splash a lot, including LSV's Mardu splash and his UB Paradox Shaper deck.

**Blue is the best colour.** Its best commons and uncommons are its card-draw and counter spells.

**#872 release-week read (superseded where it conflicts with the ranking above):**

**Hosts most positive**
- **GW Vigorbloom (lifegain / growing creatures).** It has the strongest gold commons and uncommons: Bloombrute (A−, "this card's busted"), Vigorbloom Charm B+ and Vigorbloom Vanguard B+/B. The mono-colour lifegain support is deep: Titanbones, Unflinching Hortimancer, Greenhouse Propagator and Thalia, the Survivor. It overlaps with WB lifegain. *(#874: several of these lifegain support cards are poor in Sealed.)*
- **BR Stingerquill (face burn / non-combat damage).** It gets Stingerquill Charm B+ ("basically a Lightning Bolt") plus both colours' deep removal. Tomik, Izzet Sparkmage is its build-around.
- **RW Ajani (+1/+1 counters, aggro "with reckless abandon").** Its cards are Mabel, Valley Hero, Warrior's Blades, Koth of the Homestead, Yoshimaru, Beloved Companion and Danitha. *(#874: counters theme weak, Mabel underperformed.)*
- **WB Liliana (attrition, small creatures, graveyard).** Twisted Fates (A−) is the best gold uncommon at release, but it's WWB. Blessed Ghoul is "the glue that holds the entire set together." *(#874: WB is the worst pair so far.)*
- **BG Garruk ("bestiary": deathtouch + trample).** Hapatra, the Desert Fang A− and Primal Witchstalker B.

**Middle**
- **WU Fatehold (surveil aggro).** Fatehold Charm B, Desperate Futurescribe B, good flyers. *(#874: best deck.)*
- **UB Theorix (self-mill to threshold).** Theorix Charm B, Recursive Recruitment B ("deck defining"); Paradox Shaper is a loop build-around. *(#874: #2.)*

**Hosts skeptical**
- **UG Jace's Mastery (keep empowering, walkers gain abilities).** It's fragile: "the fancier the deck, the likelier it does nothing." Kiora of Salt and Sand is the payoff.
- **RG Konstrari (Heartwood-token ramp).** Konstrari Charm C, Konstrari Improviser C−/C. It only works if Craftwork Crusher (B+/B, RRGG) and the big green top end arrive. *(#874: ramp works and Improviser is good. #873 added top end: Aerid Konstrari A+, Verdant Kraken, Hungering Puppetbeast.)*
- **UR Chandra's Prowess (noncreature spells).** Saheeli, Jewel of Avishkar B+, Clash of Elements B; the hosts distrust prowess creatures as a plan. *(#874: confirmed.)*

## Card tips

Commons/uncommons are graded in #872; rares/mythics (and the bonus-sheet "special guests") in #873, listed in a separate block per colour. **874:** marks a post-play take, which wins on conflict. Cards within each block are ordered best to worst by their release grade.

### White

- **Your Fate Ends Here** — B. Instant: kill a creature or planeswalker with MV 3+, surveil 1. Because of the MV floor you always trade even or better.
- **Thalia, the Survivor** — B. 4-mana 3/4 lifelink that taxes opposing noncreature spells. It supports lifegain and counters. **874:** A fine playable you'd never cut from white, but nothing exciting. "Not a card you need to go after actively."
- **Way of the Healer** — B. Empower Jace 5, and your walkers gain −2 to make a 2/2 Cadet and surveil 1. You usually make the 2/2 right away.
- **Saheeli, Consul of Oversight** — B. 5-mana 4/4 flyer that makes a Thopter when you scry or surveil. Jace's −1 triggers it immediately.
- **Memory Trap** — C+/B → B. The 3-mana O-Ring-style reprint: "these always play great."
- **Koth of the Homestead** — B (maybe B+; the captions garble the final call). 3-mana 2/3; landfall gains 1 life and Plains add +1/+1 counters. Enables several themes.
- **Yoshimaru, Beloved Companion** — build-around B. Counter doubler that combos with Koth and Danitha. LSV thinks it's fine in an average white deck too.
- **Teyo, Lightshield Expert** — B−. 2-mana flash 1/1: hexproof plus a +1/+1 counter (or a loyalty counter). Saves a creature and adds stats.
- **Danitha, Sword of Hope** — C+/B− (LSV "B− just flat"). 2/2 first strike that draws when you cast equipment or target your own creature. **874:** "No, I'm good." A really bad fit for the format; you're trying to kill their stuff, not cast equipment and pump spells.
- **Rescue Girl, First Responder** — C/B− (LSV B−, build-around). 1/3 flyer that bounces your own permanent on your turn, for ETB value or to replay a land. **874:** **Split.** 17Lands rates it about D-level. LSV calls it more like a build-around B: he went 7 wins in early access in RW with about 8 good bounce targets (Ways, Warrior's Blades, Mindseeker Oculus, ETB creatures, Lich's Relic), and it can pick up a land you'd otherwise miss. Marshall: "I respect that card," kill it on sight. It's bad only in decks with nothing to bounce.
- **Hexhaven Battalion** — C → C+ (LSV B−). Three 2/2 Cadets plus Empower Jace 2 for 6, or basic landcycling 2. Marshall first misread it as two tokens. LSV would happily play three. **874:** Held up. It's a tapland that's also a spell late. All the landcyclers are good.
- **Tomik, Orzhov Lawmage** — C+. 2-mana 2/1 flyer that protects walkers (only one attacker at a time) with random upside.
- **Generous Revival** — C+. Reanimate MV ≤3 with a counter, flashback 4. Wants 0–1 copies.
- **Prophesied End** — C. 2-mana instant kill, but the controller draws unless the creature was attacking. It's a solid C as "kill an attacker" and D−/F otherwise.
- **Surgical Precision** — C. Sorcery: kill a toughness-4+ creature and gain life, or draw and gain 2. It has no losing case; sorcery speed keeps it off C+.
- **Unflinching Hortimancer** — C. 2/1 ward 1 that grows on lifegain; a good common payoff for GW. **874:** Sealed: "just don't have this card in your deck."
- **Campus Crier** — C. 2-mana 3/1; exile it from the graveyard later for Empower Jace 2.
- **Graft Surgeon** — C. A 3/3 for 3 that passes its counters on when it dies. **874:** Avoid it. The +1/+1-counter theme is among the weaker things you can be doing, and exile/lockdown removal strands the counters. "Happy when my opponents play it."
- **Blossom-Blessed Angel** — C. 4-mana 2/4 flying vigilance that enters prepared with Seed Suture. "Ludicrous at common, and yet a C."
- **Yuriko, Blade of the Mighty** — C. High variance: it plays like D or B+ depending on the board. Beware best-case thinking.
- **Fateshaper Aspirant** — C (slides to D). Returns a legendary card from the graveyard, or gives a counter plus indestructible. It's a 23rd card without good legendary targets. **874:** There are tons of legends, so the return mode usually hits. LSV still calls it D-level in draft, better in Sealed and in decks with valuable legends.
- **Way of the Mentor** — build-around C. Empower Jace 5, and lifegain adds loyalty. It doesn't affect the board.
- **Academic Ascent** — C−. +2/+2 flying trick plus Empower Jace 2.
- **Shatterwing Pegasus** — C−. Fine 3-mana 2/3 flyer, but unclear where it fits in a synergy set.
- **Ghalta the Immovable** — C−/D+. 0/7 that costs 9 minus your greatest toughness. It's stuck in hand without 4–5-toughness creatures. **874:** (?) A "Galta" is cited as a Sealed card to avoid; which Ghalta is unclear.
- **Predictive Preparations** — D+. Two +1/+1 counters, flashback 4. Needs a counters payoff.
- **Refute Destiny** — sideboard. Exile a green or blue creature or walker. Don't maindeck it in Bo1.

### Blue

- **Murmuring Volume** — D. 3-mana rock with a loot; RG has better ramp. "Prove it." **874:** **Up.** In a long 3+-colour format the rummage gets used a lot. A 3-mana rock "on a generational run".
- **Living Library** — no letter grade; clearly negative ("Nah. Nope. I would not do this").


**Colourless rares & mythics (#873)**

- **Codie, Ravenous Codex** — build-around C+. Copies prepared spells; wants about 3 self-re-preparing creatures.
- **The Echoverse Fulcrum** — C. A telegraphed 5-mana wrath; you're down a card to cast it.
- **Karn, Argent Defender** — C−/D. Stops ETB triggers; LSV would consider maindecking it when your own deck isn't hurt.
- **Emrakul, the Exigent Doom** — F.

### Lands

- **Dual lands (all 10: Fatehold / Theorix / Stingerquill / Konstrari / Vigorbloom Annex + the five enemy "Commons")** — C+. Enters tapped unless you control a planeswalker, which is a real condition in this set. Graded once for the whole cycle ("one for each color pair"). "If you're heavy on playables, these lands go up in value." **874:** Held up at C+ (Innovative Commons cited). Meticulous Commons is "less enticing" because WB is the worst pair.
- **Room of Refuge** — C+. Tapped any-colour land; late game, 5 and sac for two +1/+1 counters.
- **Hexhaven Dueling Arena** — F. Colourless land with prepare activations. "I would bet the win rate is negative."
