## Format speed / meta read

- **Main mechanic: Empower Jace N.** Put N loyalty counters on your Jace token, creating it first if you don't have one. The token has **−1: surveil 1** and **−3: draw a card**. It's on "a ton of cards" in every colour, and empower stacks. Empower 4+ cashes in for a card immediately; Empower 2 does not. LSV normally dislikes complex mechanics but says this one is "so intuitive" it "gets a pass."
- **Every deck will have planeswalkers,** so "planeswalker matters" cards are real at common. Examples: the dual lands, Tomik, Orzhov Lawmage, Mabel, Bitter Recluse (removes counters, "kill that Jace"), and Archive Arbiter.
- **Jace decisions are the skill test.** Do you attack the Jace, clear blockers to reach it, or ignore it? **Sequencing tip:** cast spells *before* combat. If the opponent answers with Countersculpt (which makes a Jace), you can still attack the new Jace.
- **Prepare returns.** Each allied pair shares one prepare spell: Peer Review WU, Omit Variables UB, Vicious Verse BR, Soul Tether RG, Seed Suture GW. The hosts like that this reduces how much you track. **Threshold** (7+ cards in graveyard) lives in UB.
- **Speed: fast-ish, removal-rich.** "Games are fast," so cheap bounce and 1-drops gain value. Sticky 1-drops "overperform in modern Limited." There's a lot of mana-efficient removal, especially black (Last Gasp, Multiply by Zero, Break Under Pressure, Extended Absence) and red (No Admittance, Fulminous Forte, Wrath of the Bloodmane, Violent Echoes). Blue removal is "graded on a curve": Infinite Coursework is "about as good as blue gets."
- **High-synergy set,** so off-plan cards with fine stats get marked down (Budding Insurgent, Shatterwing Pegasus, both Yargles, Rampart Hunter). "This is not an artifact set": there are about 8 artifacts.
- **Card draw overflows.** So many cards empower Jace that pure card-draw spells (Protege's Awakening, Sphinx's Approach, Way of the Mind Sculptor) should be capped at one or two.
- **Land cyclers are good.** "We have a lot more fun when the land cycling cards are good." The basic-landcycling commons sit at C+ to B− (Vinelasher Adept, Hexhaven Battalion, Undulating Witness, Awaken the Inferno, Apex Witchstalker).
- **Overall:** both hosts are very positive: "right back to where we want to be." One predicts the set ends up graded A− or better.

## Archetypes

All ten pairs are supported ("not a five archetype set"). Allied pairs are the five **Hexhaven** schools (3 gold uncommons + 1 gold common each). Enemy pairs are the five **planeswalker** decks (2 gold uncommons + 1 common each). There's heavy hybrid mana in the gold section, and LSV says it "makes me feel like all 10 archetypes at least are getting a good shot." There's no Sunset Show yet, so this is the **release-week read, grouped rather than ranked**:

**Hosts most positive**
- **GW Vigorbloom (lifegain / growing creatures).** It has the strongest gold commons and uncommons: Bloombrute (A−, "this card's busted"), Vigorbloom Charm B+ and Vigorbloom Vanguard B+/B. The mono-colour lifegain support is deep: Titanbones, Unflinching Hortimancer, Greenhouse Propagator and Thalia, the Survivor. It overlaps with WB lifegain.
- **BR Stingerquill (face burn / non-combat damage).** It gets Stingerquill Charm B+ ("basically a Lightning Bolt") plus both colours' deep removal. Tomik, Izzet Sparkmage is its build-around.
- **RW Ajani (+1/+1 counters, aggro "with reckless abandon").** It "can't fail that badly." Its cards are Mabel, Valley Hero, Warrior's Blades, Koth of the Homestead, Yoshimaru, Beloved Companion and Danitha.
- **WB Liliana (attrition, small creatures, graveyard).** Twisted Fates (A−) is the best gold uncommon at release, but it's WWB. Blessed Ghoul is "the glue that holds the entire set together."
- **BG Garruk ("bestiary": deathtouch + trample).** Hapatra, the Desert Fang A− and Primal Witchstalker B.

**Middle**
- **WU Fatehold (surveil aggro).** Solid gold cards (Fatehold Charm B, Desperate Futurescribe B) and good flyers.
- **UB Theorix (self-mill to threshold).** Theorix Charm B and Recursive Recruitment B ("deck defining"). Paradox Shaper is a loop build-around. It depends on having enough self-mill.

**Hosts skeptical**
- **UG Jace's Mastery (keep empowering, walkers gain abilities).** It could be good but is fragile: "the fancier the deck, the likelier it does nothing." Kiora of Salt and Sand is the payoff. The expensive Ways and Compel Brutality convert loyalty into board.
- **RG Konstrari (Heartwood-token ramp).** Konstrari Charm C ("this thing sucks"), Konstrari Improviser C−/C. It only works if Craftwork Crusher (B+/B, RRGG) and the big green top end arrive.
- **UR Chandra's Prowess (noncreature spells).** Saheeli, Jewel of Avishkar B+ and Clash of Elements B are strong, but the hosts distrust prowess creatures as a plan.

## Card tips

Grades are from #872 (release). Cards within each colour are ordered best to worst.

### White

- **Your Fate Ends Here** — B. Instant: kill a creature or planeswalker with MV 3+, surveil 1. Because of the MV floor you always trade even or better.
- **Thalia, the Survivor** — B. 4-mana 3/4 lifelink that taxes opposing noncreature spells. It supports lifegain and counters.
- **Way of the Healer** — B. Empower Jace 5, and your walkers gain −2 to make a 2/2 Cadet and surveil 1. You usually make the 2/2 right away.
- **Saheeli, Consul of Oversight** — B. 5-mana 4/4 flyer that makes a Thopter when you scry or surveil. Jace's −1 triggers it immediately.
- **Memory Trap** — C+/B → B. The 3-mana O-Ring-style reprint: "these always play great."
- **Koth of the Homestead** — B (maybe B+; the captions garble the final call). 3-mana 2/3; landfall gains 1 life and Plains add +1/+1 counters. Enables several themes.
- **Yoshimaru, Beloved Companion** — build-around B. Counter doubler that combos with Koth and Danitha. LSV thinks it's fine in an average white deck too.
- **Teyo, Lightshield Expert** — B−. 2-mana flash 1/1: hexproof plus a +1/+1 counter (or a loyalty counter). Saves a creature and adds stats.
- **Danitha, Sword of Hope** — C+/B− (LSV "B− just flat"). 2/2 first strike that draws when you cast equipment or target your own creature.
- **Rescue Girl, First Responder** — C/B− (LSV B−, build-around). 1/3 flyer that bounces your own permanent on your turn, for ETB value or to replay a land.
- **Hexhaven Battalion** — C → C+ (LSV B−). Three 2/2 Cadets plus Empower Jace 2 for 6, or basic landcycling 2. Marshall first misread it as two tokens. LSV would happily play three.
- **Tomik, Orzhov Lawmage** — C+. 2-mana 2/1 flyer that protects walkers (only one attacker at a time) with random upside.
- **Generous Revival** — C+. Reanimate MV ≤3 with a counter, flashback 4. Wants 0–1 copies.
- **Prophesied End** — C. 2-mana instant kill, but the controller draws unless the creature was attacking. It's a solid C as "kill an attacker" and D−/F otherwise.
- **Surgical Precision** — C. Sorcery: kill a toughness-4+ creature and gain life, or draw and gain 2. It has no losing case; sorcery speed keeps it off C+.
- **Unflinching Hortimancer** — C. 2/1 ward 1 that grows on lifegain; a good common payoff for GW.
- **Campus Crier** — C. 2-mana 3/1; exile it from the graveyard later for Empower Jace 2.
- **Graft Surgeon** — C. A 3/3 for 3 that passes its counters on when it dies.
- **Blossom-Blessed Angel** — C. 4-mana 2/4 flying vigilance that enters prepared with Seed Suture. "Ludicrous at common, and yet a C."
- **Yuriko, Blade of the Mighty** — C. High variance: it plays like D or B+ depending on the board. Beware best-case thinking.
- **Fateshaper Aspirant** — C (slides to D). Returns a legendary card from the graveyard, or gives a counter plus indestructible. It's a 23rd card without good legendary targets.
- **Way of the Mentor** — build-around C. Empower Jace 5, and lifegain adds loyalty. It doesn't affect the board.
- **Academic Ascent** — C−. +2/+2 flying trick plus Empower Jace 2.
- **Shatterwing Pegasus** — C−. Fine 3-mana 2/3 flyer, but unclear where it fits in a synergy set.
- **Ghalta the Immovable** — C−/D+. 0/7 that costs 9 minus your greatest toughness. It's stuck in hand without 4–5-toughness creatures.
- **Predictive Preparations** — D+. Two +1/+1 counters, flashback 4. Needs a counters payoff.
- **Refute Destiny** — sideboard. Exile a green or blue creature or walker. Don't maindeck it in Bo1.

### Blue

- **Arni, Humble Scribe** — B+/B. 3-mana 3/2 looter that untaps whenever another nontoken creature enters. The only knock is 2 toughness.
- **Mindseeker Oculus** — B. 3-mana 2/1 plus Empower Jace 4: a creature, a card and a leftover walker. "Absurd" for a common.
- **Traxos, Academy Guardian** — B. 1/5 flying vigilance prowess that costs 2 less after a noncreature spell. It's often a 2-mana wall that also attacks.

- **Plan for All Outcomes** — B/C+. 4-mana enchantment that tucks any nonland permanent. It's real removal for blue, and empowers Jace on your first noncreature spell each turn.
- **Infinite Coursework** — C+/B. 3-mana lockdown aura (tap, loses abilities, doesn't untap). "About as good as blue gets."
- **Tetsuko Umezawa, Fugitive** — B−. Makes power-or-toughness ≤1 creatures unblockable. The Dominaria version overperformed.
- **Ruric Thar, Biomagus** — B−. 6-mana 4/6 flyer with double prowess that draws when an opponent targets it.
- **Precise Redaction** — sideboard B. Counter a white or black spell.
- **Geist of Saint Thalia** — build-around C+. 2-mana 1/2 flyer that discounts your noncreature spells.
- **Hapatra, the Desert Frost** — C+. 4-mana 4/3; tap-and-stun on ETB. 3 toughness is shaky.
- **Undulating Witness** — C+. 5-mana 3/5 flyer with basic landcycling 2.
- **Icy Reception** — C/C+. A soft Essence Scatter that also hits legendary spells, or −5/−0.
- **Surveillance Phantasm** — C/C+. 2-mana 2/3 defender flyer that attacks after you surveil; "looks pushed."

- **Yuriko, Hope from the Shadows** — C−/C+ (?). 1-mana flash 1/1: −X/−0 or surveil 2. Marshall was "talked up to a C−". LSV is high on it and says it's good to rebuy with Fateshaper Aspirant.

- **Semester Foreseer** — C. 4-mana 3/4 that surveils and enters prepared (Peer Review). The hosts note that SOS's similar Campus Composer underperformed.
- **Yargle, Goliath of Otaria** — sideboard C/C− vs R/G; D/F main. Vanilla 3/9.
- **Cryotheory Adept** — D/C−. 2-mana 2/1 prowess with a graveyard tap/stun mode.
- **Perfected Theory** — D. 1-mana: base P/T becomes 1/1 or 4/5. It needs combat.
- **Sphinx's Approach** — D. UU1 draw 2 is too expensive; the Sphinx-tutor mode needs multiple copies.
- **Way of the Cryomancer** — D. Its −3 (copy your next instant or sorcery) is rarely better than Jace's own −3 draw.
- **Fblthp, Impossibly Lost** — D− (joking "build-around A+" for the alt-win). A cost-reduced Divination that needs combat damage.
- **Proft, Consulting Detective** — no grade given. Pay 2 on scry/surveil for a +1/+1 counter and a card. Wants about 4+ surveil sources, which most decks have. "Sweet card."

### Black

- **Proft, Sinister Mastermind** — B. A 3-mana 5/5 menace castable only at threshold, or discard it for −3/−1. Both halves are good.
- **Extended Absence** — B+ → B. 4-mana instant exile of a creature or walker plus drain 1.
- **Massacre Girl, Most Wanted** — B. 5-mana 4/4 must-answer that drains on your deaths and grows on noncombat damage.
- **Gallia, Tragic Host** — B−. 2-mana 2/1 menace that recurs from the graveyard (4B, exile another creature card).
- **Terminal Criticism** — sideboard B. Destroy a blue or red creature or walker.
- **Apex Witchstalker** — C+/B−. 6-mana 6/4 menace that gains 2 on ETB and death, with basic landcycling.
- **Screeching Soulbreaker** — C+. 1/4 flyer that drains 1 on attack.
- **Rewrite Regrets** — C+. Reanimate MV ≤6 plus Empower Jace 2; better with the self-mill.
- **Winter, Tormented Loner** — C / build-around C+. 0/3 whose ETB is an edict (it can sac itself). Needs fodder.

- **Loot, the Anomaly** — D. The threshold-gated sac isn't reliable, and there's no real sacrifice archetype.
- **Rampart Hunter** — D. Looks classic, but it has no synergy.
- **Teyo, Diamondblade Mage** — D (maybe D+). The deathtouch and the counter overlap, and 1 toughness hurts.
- **Solve for Disappointment** — D− (started at D). Missing is a disaster.
- **Yargle, Glutton of Urborg** — D/F. "It does not play well."

- **Edgar, Ancient Bloodlord** — B−/B. 2-mana 2/3 sac outlet that gains life.
- **Blessed Ghoul** — C+/C. Hybrid lifelink recursion. Marshall: "the glue that holds the entire set together."

**UR Chandra**
- **Saheeli, Jewel of Avishkar** — B+. "Basically a Murmuring Mystic" with hasty Thopters off any noncreature spell. "Don't let them untap with Saheeli."
- **Clash of Elements** — B. 3-mana instant that answers any nonland permanent (top of library + 2 damage, or bottom).
- **Twinned Vision** — C+/C− (speakers unclear). The discard-a-card flashback cost "really dents it."

**BG Garruk**
- **Hapatra, the Desert Fang** — B+/A− (LSV A−, "it's just always going to work"). 5-mana 3/3 that places −1/−1 counters equal to the greatest MV in your graveyard.
- **Primal Witchstalker** — B (maybe B+). 3-mana 2/1 menace that mills 4 and returns a land untapped.
- **Ferocity of the Hunt** — C−/C. Flash deathtouch aura that returns the creature when it dies. "Normally a very solid D, so that's a compliment."

**RW Ajani**
- **Mabel, Valley Hero** — B/B+ (speakers unclear). Every creature that enters gets a +1/+1 counter; a must-kill on turn 3.
- **Warrior's Blades** — B. A Lightning Helix, then decent equipment. "Would play multiple copies."
- **Charge the Sanctum** — C/C−. Hybrid team pump; risks a blowout at 3 mana.

**UG Jace**
- **Mind Meanderer** — B−/B (LSV B). 6-mana 4/4 flyer that fights; a 2-for-1 vs small and medium creatures.
- **Kiora of Salt and Sand** — build-around B/B− (A− floated first). Gives your walkers −8: an 8/8 hexproof Leviathan.
- **Tam's Resistance** — C. Counter + vigilance, then Empower Jace 4. Needs a creature target.

### Artifacts

- **Traxos, Scourge Eternal** — B/C+. 4-mana 5/4 trample that untaps when you cast an artifact or creature spell, which is trivially easy. Stomps Jaces.
- **Eye of Jace** — build-around C (Marshall, "prove it") / C+ (LSV). Surveils each upkeep, then drains 2 at threshold. LSV says it has enough homes (UB, BG, prowess) to take on spec.
- **Archive Arbiter** — C+. 6-mana 4/4 flyer: destroy a noncreature, nonland permanent (walkers!) or gain 4. "One goes a long way."
- **Keeper of the Quiet Hour** — C/C−. 3-mana 3/2 plus Empower Jace 2. Good for a colourless common.
- **Medic's Kitesail** — D+/C−. +1/+0, flying and lifegain; LSV thinks it'll be main-decked more than it looks.
- **Afterthought Sentry** — D. Maybe a sideboard card.
- **Murmuring Volume** — D. 3-mana rock with a loot; RG has better ramp. "Prove it."
- **Living Library** — no letter grade; clearly negative ("Nah. Nope. I would not do this").

### Lands

- **Dual lands (all 10: Fatehold / Theorix / Stingerquill / Konstrari / Vigorbloom Annex + the five enemy "Commons")** — C+. "If you're heavy on playables, these lands go up in value."
- **Room of Refuge** — C+. Tapped any-colour land; late game, 5 and sac for two +1/+1 counters.
- **Hexhaven Dueling Arena** — F. Colourless land with prepare activations. "I would bet the win rate is negative."
