## Format speed / meta read

- **Small set, five archetypes, designed for Pick-Two Draft** (4-player pods) — same structural mold as Spider-Man and TMNT. Two allied pairs (WU, BR) and three enemy pairs (BG, RW, GU).
- **The dominant evaluation lens this episode is the mana-exchange test:** "how would I feel if my opponent used a removal spell that costs less than this card to remove it?" LR applied it to nearly every card and it is the single best predictor of their grades. Five-mana creatures that need to untap before doing anything got punished hard — Wilderland Scrounger D+, Iron Hills Stalwart D, Misty Mountains Raider C+, Glóin the Mighty C+.
- **Corollary — cheap creatures that replace themselves grade above rate.** Desolation Prowler (B+) and Bilbo Baggins, Burglar (B) are the two clearest cases.
- **Storied is easy to switch on.** The storied card itself counts toward the three, so you only need two more artifacts/legends/sagas — and treasure tokens count. LSV: "it's not looking that hard right now." Don't grade storied cards as build-arounds.
- **Amass is worth less than a token of the same size.** LSV, explicitly, closing black: "you should discount amass goblins — it is worse than just getting a 2/2 or a 3/3. It's not bad. It's just not as good as that." Sacrificing between amass triggers is usually better than stacking counters.
- **Blue is not assertive.** It wants to ramp or draw extra cards; LR flatly did not believe in a blue beatdown deck. The draw-two payoffs (Lakeshore Apothecary, Master's Councillors, Lake-town Toymaker) all landed C-range and all need real enabler density.
- **White is the go-wide, low-curve color** — Dwarven Provisioner, Thorin's Last Stand, Moment of Glory, Eagle of the Great Shelf, Esgaroth Garrison all pay off creature count. But LR noted the set deliberately does **not** go very wide (the goblin armies are one body), so the payoffs may be under-supported.
- **Splashing looks bad and the fixing is a trap.** LR saw no gold cards pulling toward 3+ colors, and graded Old Thrush D specifically because bad fixing misleads inexperienced players.
- **Adventure heuristic:** compare the adventure half's cost to the creature's. If the adventure is *cheaper* you'll get both halves nearly every time (Bilbo Baggins Burglar, Gollum Silent Slinker) — a real grade bump. If it's *more expensive* (Bilbo, Luckwearer's Burglar's Plot) you're usually giving the spell half up.
- **Sealed-specific:** LR called out **Sound the Trumpets** as D+ in draft but **C+ in Sealed** ("sealed matters a lot these days"), and **Master's Councillors** as potentially excellent in Sealed. The general shape — slower games, more room for reactive cards — applies.
- Vibe check: both hosts ended positive. "Refreshing to be in a normal fantasy environment," Core-Set-like simplicity, an actual vanilla creature. Caveat: small sets historically have short runway.

## Archetypes

Five supported pairs, not ten. LR did **not** rank them — no gameplay data existed. What follows is their read on each from the signpost cards.

### Blue-White Humans — recruit
Signposts: **Bard the Bowman** (B), **Patient Instructor** (C+), **Eagle's Rescue** (B). Recruit gives looting to white and leaves a 1/1 behind on a nonland discard, which feeds the go-wide payoffs and the draw-your-second-card cards. LSV on Eagle's Rescue: an aura that makes every token a 3/3 flier, so you have to be assertive. LR were positive on the shell but skeptical of the draw-two payoffs in isolation.

### Black-Red Goblins — amass + sacrifice
Signposts: **Goblin Plate Mail** (C/C+), **Fearsome Goblin Pair** (B−), **Bolg of the North** (C+). The deck wants sacrifice outlets because amass pays you for re-making the Army rather than growing it. **Snowslope Hunter** (B) is a free, no-mana sac outlet that turns Army tokens into cards — LR were highest on it. Bolg was the disappointment: LSV thinks it's much harder to line up than it reads.

### Black-Green Wolves — ferocious
Signposts: **Duskwatch Hunter** (C/C+), **The Chief Warg** (B), **Large Bear** (B−). Every ferocious trigger in the episode was an *attack* trigger, so the deck has to be able to attack profitably. **Desolation Prowler** (B+) is the best card in the archetype and the highest-graded card of the review — a 2-mana putrid leech that turns ferocious on by itself.

### Red-White Dwarves — storied + equipment
Signposts: **Nori, Teller of Tales** (C), **Thorin Oakenshield** (C+), **Bifur, Melodic Rider** (B). LR expect storied to come on naturally in this deck. **Dwarven Mattock** is a build-around B *inside* Dwarves and a D outside it — the equipment payoffs live entirely in this one archetype.

### Green-Blue Elves — landfall + ramp
Signposts: **Mirkwood Nurturer** (C/C−), **Silvan Reveler** (B), **Thranduil, Sindarin Liege** (B+). Thranduil was the highest-graded signpost in the set — 3-mana Mulch on the adventure half, then an elf lord that makes a token on every landfall. Marshall: "blue green looks incredible." LSV's counter, and the one to weight: "blue-green misses a lot of the time — it's one of the decks that misses the most often, up to and including the last Lord of the Rings."

## Card tips

### White

- **Celebrate the Mountain-king** — B+ (#865 release). Four-mana O-Ring that also recruits. Highest-graded white card.
- **The Mountain-king's Return** — B. Saga: recruit → raise dead (MV 3 or less) → +1/+1 counter. Needs a critical mass of 1–3 drops.
- **Dáin, Lord of the Iron Hills** — B−. 2-mana 2/2 vigilance dwarf with storied; the Propaganda half is a bonus, not the reason.
- **Iron Hills Blacksmith** — B−. 1/1 double strike that makes an Axe token — artifact for storied, and it hits for 4 once equipped.
- **Stone by Sunlight** — B−. Modal: destroy a power-4+ creature, or give indestructible. Always trades up on mana.
- **Lake-town Toymaker** — B−. 3/4 that gives +3/+0 and first strike at beginning of combat if you've drawn two. Build-around; needs free recruit effects.
- **Eagle of the Great Shelf** — C/C+. 2/5 flier that pumps per other creature. Meets the bar for white decks despite the 5-mana risk.
- **Bofur, Reliable Guardian** — C. 1-mana 1/1 lifelink legend + hexproof/indestructible adventure. C+ in the storied decks.
- **Lake-town Lookout** — C. 1/1 that recruits on death. Low impact, but one-drops that do anything are hard to underrate.
- **Dwarven Provisioner** — C. 2/2 with a 4-mana team pump. "You'll see it on the battlefield all the time."
- **Dwarven Shortsword** — C. Four mana for a 3/4 that leaves a +1/+2 sword behind.
- **Velvetwing Butterflies** — C. 2/2 flier + instant adventure that taps one or two creatures.
- **Esgaroth Garrison** — C. */5 for five that recruits; power equals creature count.
- **Magnificent End** — C. Five damage for 5, or 2 if the target is tapped.
- **Moment of Glory** — C (opened C−). Flashback team pump; the card you *want* to discard to recruit.
- **Thorin's Last Stand** — C (opened C−). Modal team pump / disenchant + 2 life. Can just win a go-wide game.
- **Ori, Keeper of Songs** — C−. 3/3 legendary dwarf with storied; you play it to *be* a storied enabler.
- **Vow to Erebor** — D. Untap + 2/+2 for two is not enough without the equipment rider mattering.

### Blue

- **Bilbo, Luckwearer** — B. Two-mana unblockable looter; Burglar's Plot is upside you usually forgo (adventure costs more than the creature).
- **Bilbo Baggins, Burglar** — B. **Best common in the set.** Three-mana 2/1 that draws a card, plus a Scry 2 adventure you almost always get. LR compared it to Hero in Training (an A−).
- **Old Fat Spider Can't See Me** — B / B− (split). Four-chapter saga: hexproof → fog a creature indefinitely → draw → draw.
- **Master's Councillors** — C+ (opened D). 1/3 vigilance that mills 3 on your second draw each turn. Realistically a 2-mana 3/3 vigilance; excellent in Sealed.
- **Plunder the Trollshaws** — C+. Think Twice. Triggers the draw-two payoffs on both halves, and the flashback works on their turn.
- **Enchanted River's Grasp** — C+. Blue's clean removal aura. Taps, strips counters, removes abilities — templated specifically to kill Armies.
- **Uneasy Partings** — C+. Put a creature on top or bottom; costs 1 less against attacking nontokens. Solid blue playable.
- **Long Lake Nuisance** — C. Four-mana 3/1 flier with recruit. Good stat line for a flier.
- **Lake-town Mariners** — C. 6/5 vigilance ward 2 + Gone Fishing (blink two of your permanents at instant speed). Two mediocre halves that add up.
- **Thranduil's Decree** — C. Six-mana counter that lets you cast a countered permanent free. LR: "it's going to be an F or an A, not in between." Much worse in Bo1.
- **Confusticate and Bebother** — C−. Force Spike-for-4 or draw two discard one. Careful with reactive card counts.
- **Elven Raft-Steerer** — C−. 3/2 with landfall tap-or-untap. Blue isn't assertive enough to want the tempo.
- **Lakeshore Apothecary** — build-around C. 1/2 vigilance that grows on your second draw each turn. D outside a heavy recruit deck.
- **Gandalf, Wandering Wizard** — D/D+. Five-mana 4/5 ward 3 with a 6-mana "shuffle me in, draw 3." Rarely a responsible use of 11 mana.
- **Mirkwood Meditator** — D. 2/4 landfall that can become a 4/2. Lost between archetypes.
- **Ravenhill Flock** — D. **Trap.** 1/2 flier that grows on every draw. LR: "these basically never work."
- **Elvenking's Harper** — D. 2/2 with a 5-mana unblockable activation.
- **Sound the Trumpets** — D+ draft, **C+ in Sealed**. Cancel that recruits off cheap spells. Needs three other instant-speed things to leave up.

### Black

- **Desolation Prowler** — B+. **Highest-graded card in the review.** Putrid Leech that's easier to cast; turns ferocious on by itself and sits on the right side of the mana exchange.
- **Nighthowl Pursuer** — B. One-mana 1/1 menace that attacks as a 3/3 with ferocious on.
- **Gathering of Darkness** — B. Four-mana Gravedigger that also amasses 3. "Just really good value, reason to go into black."
- **Gnashing of Teeth** — B. Modal −5/−5 with exile, or a −1/−1 sweep. Sorcery speed is the only knock.
- **Down, Down to Goblin-town** — B. Saga: Duress → amass 1 → drain 1 → drain 1. Strictly better than Rage into the Valley.
- **Great Ugly-Looking Goblin** — B. 4/4 for six + a 2-mana amass-2 adventure; grants menace to your countered creatures.
- **Bilbo's Deadly Slice** — C+/B−. The set's Murder at 1BB. Double black is the only friction.
- **Stir Up Trouble** — C+. One-mana Destroy with an additional cost (sac an artifact/creature, or pay 4). Great with Army tokens.
- **Front Porch Sentries** — C+. Two-mana 2/2 that gives −1/−1 on death. Better than the usual 1-mana version.
- **Stony-Voiced Goblins** — C+. 1/1 that strips a card. Excellent sacrifice fodder.
- **Rage into the Valley** — C+. Draw a card, lose a life, amass 2.
- **Ravening Warg** — C. Two-mana 2/2 deathtouch that gains 2 life on a ferocious attack.
- **Gollum, Silent Slinker** — C−/D (split). 4/3 menace at common + a 1-mana adventure (counter + 2 life). You get both halves nearly always.
- **Crude Bent Blade** — C−/D (split). Equipment stapled to an Edict. LSV: edicts will be bad in a format full of tokens and Armies.
- **Gollum the Abandoned** — D+ overall, **C/C+ in an aggressive deck with sacrifice fodder.** Can't block, which is a much bigger liability than aggro drafters expect.
- **Dreaded Bat-Cloud** — C+/D+ (split). 4/2 flying deathtouch that costs 3 less if a creature died this turn. Morbid is harder to enable than it looks.
- **Great Fierce Bee** — D/D+. Three-mana 2/2 flier that scries on any creature death.
- **Reverent Howl** — D. Draw 2 lose 2, or a bad +2/+2 lifelink trick. Flexible but weak on both halves.

### Red

- **Bothersome Noisemaker** — B. Two-mana 2/2 that amasses on every noncreature spell. Kill on sight.
- **Snowslope Hunter** — B. 2/3 with a **free, no-mana sac outlet** that exiles the top card to play. Turns Armies into cards. The engine card of BR.
- **Gandalf, Spark Starter** — B. Six-mana 4/3 reach that deals 3 divided. Cleans up after a favorable attack.
- **Pinecone Strike** — B−. Two-mana instant, 3 damage with exile, plus destroy an artifact token. Red's best common removal.
- **Bombur, Gentle Dreamer** — B−. Three-mana 5/3 that doesn't untap without an enduring story. Blocks fine, auto-ferocious, and is one-third of storied.
- **Tidings of War** — C+. Amass 1, flashback for amass 3. Good to mill, good to discard, good sac fodder.
- **Dori, Bearer of Friends** — C+. Three-mana 3/2 trample legend that makes a Treasure — two-thirds of storied on one card.
- **Glóin the Mighty** — C+. 4/3 that adds R each main phase + Easy Pickings (1 damage to each opposing creature). Grade hinges entirely on how good Easy Pickings is.
- **Misty Mountains Raider** — C+. Five-mana 4/4 that amasses 2 on attack. Has to survive.
- **Goblin-town Flunkies** — C+ when you can use the goblins, C/C− when you can't.
- **Ragged Short Spear** — C. Tormenting Voice stapled to an equipment. Storied enabler.
- **Óin the Brave** — C. 1/3 legend with storied, rummage activation. "Would have been a rare a decade ago."
- **Gundabad Opportunist** — C. Four-mana 4/2 that exiles-to-play the top card. A two-for-one served up to cheap removal.
- **Burn, Burn, Tree and Fern** — C. Saga: 6 damage → destroy an artifact → add R → add R. Four-mana sorcery-speed kill spell, frontloaded.
- **Smaug, the Great Calamity** — C. Seven-mana 5/5 flying legendary dragon **at common**, with a 5-mana "deal 5 damage" adventure. LR loved the design, doubted the mana cost. Note: dies to The Black Arrow; the *mythic* Smaug does not.
- **Dwarven Mauler** — C−. One-mana 2/1 that reduces equip costs by 2. Play it if you want a 1-mana 2/1 dwarf.
- **Smaug's Fury** — D+. +3/+0, reach and first strike. Generic pump with no payoffs behind it.
- **Iron Hills Stalwart** — D. Five-mana 4/5 reach trample that reattaches an equipment. "Not interested."

### Green

- **Old Fat Spider** — B. Six-mana 6/7 reach that can't be blocked by power ≤2 and draws a card when targeted. "This is what I want to spend six mana on."
- **Nasty Little Rabbit** — B. One-mana 1/2 that grows every combat with ferocious on.
- **Troll Negotiations** — B. Two counters then fight, for 2GG. Swingy but decides games when it resolves.
- **Wood Elves** — B / C+ (unresolved split). Three-mana 1/1 that fetches an untapped Forest. Forest-only hurts; LSV suspects nostalgia inflation.
- **Woodland Weavemaster** — build-around B. Elf mana that only casts elf spells. One of your best cards in a real Elves deck, unplayable outside it.
- **Quarrel** — C+. Instant-speed bite. Reliable in a format where green creatures are big.
- **Attercop** — C/C+. Two-mana 2/1 reach deathtouch with a small landfall pump. Kills any flier.
- **Wargling** — C. Two-mana 2/2 that gives your whole team trample on a ferocious attack. Rich-get-richer.
- **Warg Tactics** — C. Modal: destroy a flier, **or** +1/+1 counter with trample and hexproof. Maindeckable despite the sideboard half.
- **Mirkwood Pathmaker** — C/C−. */* equal to lands. Keeps up, never exciting.
- **Galion, Elvenking's Butler** — C. 4/4 that copies its own stats onto another attacker. Narrower than it reads.
- **Ordinary Bear** — C/C−. Four-mana 4/5 **vanilla** — the set's throwback card.
- **Boughside Wanderers** — C. Five-mana 4/4 that digs 4 for a permanent, +2/+2 on landfall. The common ramp target.
- **Guardian of the Halls** — C/C−. Two-mana 2/2 trample with a repeatable 7-mana pump. A ramp-deck two-drop.
- **Beorn's Hospitality** — C−. Landfall counters; 7 mana to become a land-sized creature. LR skeptical.
- **Beorn, Reluctant Host** — C−/D+. Five-mana 5/5 trample + Till and Tend (extra land drop, doesn't fetch). The weakest version of that effect.
- **Wilderland Scrounger** — C+ / **D+** (big split). Five-mana 3/6 that puts a counter on each creature on a ferocious attack. LSV: "not where you want to put your mana these days."
- **Little Bear** — D. Three-mana 3/2 flash that untaps a creature; not enough bears in the set.

### Multicolor / hybrid signposts

- **Thranduil, Sindarin Liege** (GU) — B+. Elf lord + landfall token maker, with a 3-mana Mulch adventure. Highest-graded signpost.
- **Bard the Bowman** (WU) — B. 1/3 reach that puts a counter and grants lifelink on your second draw each turn. Threat of activation at instant speed.
- **Eagle's Rescue** (WU) — B. Four-mana +2/+2 flying aura that recurs itself onto a power-1-or-less creature. Turns every recruit token into a threat.
- **The Chief Warg** (BG) — B. Four-mana 3/3 menace that draws on a ferocious attack. "Must-kill, best card on the battlefield" once on.
- **Silvan Reveler** (GU) — B. 3/2 that loots-and-ramps on ETB and buys itself back with landfall. A genuine grind engine; also the perfect card to discard to recruit.
- **Bifur, Melodic Rider** (RW) — B. Six-mana 4/5 that doubles every dwarf trigger with an enduring story.
- **Fearsome Goblin Pair** (BR) — B−. 1/1 that amasses 4 when it dies. You want it to die; leans harder BR than most hybrids.
- **Large Bear** (BG) — B−. Five-mana 5/5 reach trample haste. Pure rate card, easy to cast off hybrid mana.
- **Bolg of the North** (BR) — C+. Five-mana 5/5 that sacs a creature to throw its power at another and amasses the excess. Harder to line up than it looks.
- **Thorin Oakenshield** (RW) — C+. 3/2 trample, ward 1 for your team with an enduring story. Every RW deck plays it; not a bomb.
- **Patient Instructor** (WU) — C+. Three-mana 2/2 vigilance that recruits on ETB.
- **Goblin Plate Mail** (BR) — C/C+. Two-mana equipment that amasses 1 and auto-attaches. Flexible enough to take early; equip 4 is real.
- **Duskwatch Hunter** (BG) — C/C+. Three-mana 3/1 that can't be blocked by tokens and puts a counter on ETB. Plays as a 4-power creature.
- **Mirkwood Nurturer** (GU) — C/C−. Three-mana 3/2 that bounces a permanent for a counter. One is fine; don't play three.
- **Nori, Teller of Tales** (RW) — C. Two-mana 2/2 legend that gives an attacker first strike.

### Artifacts, colorless and lands

- **Thrór's Map** — B+ / B. Two-mana legendary artifact: fetch a basic to hand, then `{2}, {T}: loot`. **Play it in ~80% of decks** — only a very aggressive deck skips it.
- **Troop of Ponies** — B. Two-mana colorless 2/1 that sacs to fetch two basics (one to the battlefield). Colorless, so it goes anywhere.
- **The Black Arrow** — B−. Three-mana flash equipment: 1 damage to any target, destroys a dragon outright, then +1/+1 and reach for 1. "Mostly a C+, but an A when they have a dragon."
- **Hobbit Hole** — C+. Sac to fetch a basic; halflingcycling 4 finds one of the set's nine halflings. Free inclusion, like Evolving Wilds.
- **Common dual land cycle** — C/C+. Lake-town (WU), Goblin Town (BR), Mirkwood (BG), Iron Hills (RW), Elvenking's Halls (GU). Enter tapped, and sac for two +1/+1 counters on the pair's tribe.
- **Long-Bodied Grey Dog** — C. Three-mana 2/2 flash reach that makes a Treasure.
- **Dwarven Mattock** — build-around **B in Dwarves, D outside.** Auto-attaches to a dwarf; +2/+2 and ward 1. Equip 3 for everyone else is too slow.
- **Giant's Boulder** — D. Scry 2, mana filter, 7-mana sac-to-destroy. Fringe playable; counts for storied.
- **Well-Worn Spatula** — D. One-mana equipment, gain 2, +1/+1, equip 1. Playable only if you need an artifact or a 4-power enabler.
- **Old Thrush** — D. Two-mana 1/2 flier, gain 2, tutor a basic **to the top**. LR: bad fixing as a trap is actively harmful design.
- **Key to the Side-Door** — **F**. Unblockable activation, or discard a redundant legend to draw two. "Your win rate is just going to be low."
