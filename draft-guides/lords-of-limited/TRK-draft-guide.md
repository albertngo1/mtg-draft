## Format speed / meta read

Updated format truths from episode 504, with the Crash Course truths that 504 did not overturn:

1. **Back to basics: play to the board better than your opponent.** This is "level one". "Level two",
   when both players are on board, is burying the opponent in two-for-ones late. There are many
   two-for-ones at common and uncommon (from Beam Me Up) and at rare. Expect to give and absorb several.
   You are not out of a game because you were three-for-one'd once.
2. **Beam Me Up ranges "from bargain to blight", and is closer to upside.** Beam Me Up is flashback on
   sorceries only, with the extra cost of returning a creature you control to your hand. It needs a
   board, and needs you to be ahead or at parity, to spend a turn on. Best are the self-timed card-advantage
   ones: **Open Communications**, **Pattern Buffer Preservation**, then **There's Coffee in that Nebula**
   and **Boarding Action**. Worse are the ones whose timing the opponent's board decides (Energize). The
   best pickup targets are creatures with good ETBs (Constable Odo, Fugitive Listener).
3. **Don't get cute.** Draft cards, then decks. Both hosts lost early drafts by latching onto
   build-arounds (Collation Trimatrix, dilemma payoffs). Cards that don't affect the board "have to be very
   good". Be intentional about non-creature slots. Removal, Beam Me Up and spacecraft eat them fast, and
   **a 12-creature deck is a trap**. Corollary: don't play *bad* cards just to be on board. Your two-drops
   need a late-game job, either staying relevant or being sacrifice fodder.
4. **Spacecraft: the good ones are great, and most of the good ones are rare or mythic.** Common and
   uncommon spacecraft "largely underwhelmed". There is no upper limit on good spacecraft, but run
   filler ones (Oberth-Class Starship, Suurok-Class Science Vessel) only if the rest of the deck is built
   to play to the board. Station swings are big (creature, creature, spacecraft online). Remember to say
   station, not crew.
5. **Three power is a notch above two power.** Station thresholds are 5, 6 and 8. A 3-power creature gets
   there in two or three taps, while a 2-power creature needs three or four. 2/1s are a good attacking
   stat line, because the format has many 3/2s and few x/3–x/4 cheap blockers.
6. **Removal is excellent.** Pack it to break up stationing turns and to kill rares. **Card advantage and
   selection are king.** Sealed flooded easily with land cyclers, Warp Core, Oberth-Class Starship and
   the mana rock, but don't cut land drops. Eight mana lets you beam up and replay Constable Odo in one turn.
7. **Fixing is everywhere, but don't play soup.** The format has a full basic-landcycler cycle (a notch
   below the FRA / Marvel ones, about C+, still fine as a 23rd card), a gain-land cycle, a common fetch,
   colourless utility lands, Warp Core and Class-F Shuttlecraft. A typical soup deck (cheap removal, set
   up mana, then play stuff) is "antithetical to everything the format is asking".

**Colour ranking (episode 504, one host):** black 1, white 2, red 3, blue 4, green 5. The other host
leans "Orzhov format" and says "Mardu" fits awkwardly. Crash Course rankings (superseded): one host
said Esper (W/U/B commons best), and the other said black 1, blue 2, green 3, white 4, red "a very
distant 5th".

## Card notes

Notes merge both episodes. **504 (11-09) overrides the Crash Course (11-03)**, and the arrow `→` marks a
flip. All are pre-release opinions. `(?)` marks a reading the transcript doesn't settle.

### White

- **Hologram Janeway** — 2W 3/3 artifact. When you attack with power 8+, put a +1/+1 counter on target creature. "This card just rules". It is a very high pick, very gluey, and works wherever you plug it in (504).
- **Malfunctioning Holodeck** — 2W artifact with ward 2 that exiles an opponent's nonland permanent ("O-Ring"). "Outstanding" (Crash Course) and called excellent removal in 504. It is "way better" than Command Decision. You can find it with There's Coffee in that Nebula.
- **U.S.S. Excelsior, Cutting Edge** — Both hosts' #1 white uncommon in the Crash Course. It is a 4-mana spacecraft (4/4 flyer at station 8) whose ETB destroys a tapped creature, and stationing creates more tapped targets. Not revisited in 504.
- **Brad Boimler, Eager Ensign** — 1W 2/2 lifelink that adds extra counters when tapped. One host's #2 white uncommon. "Just a good card", helping both stationing and incidental counters (Crash Course).
- **There's Coffee in that Nebula** — 1W impulse (top 5: a Plains or a nonland permanent with MV ≤3) with Beam Me Up. It was a Crash Course top-3 uncommon for one host. In 504 it is "excellent", a slight notch below Open Communications / Pattern Buffer Preservation because it can whiff.
- **Free Borg Revolutionaries** — 3WW landcycler artifact creature with a team +1/+1 ETB. In 504 it is played as a real card in aggressive white artifact decks, "basically closing the door" in WB.
- **Rutherford, Upgraded Ensign** — 2W 2/2 that digs 6 for an artifact. "Great value in most decks" and a good Beam Me Up target. You need about 7+ artifacts, which white reaches easily. Clearly below Hologram Janeway (504).
- **Andromedan Automatons** — 2W 3/1 that tutors the other copies. Both hosts liked it in the Crash Course, best in Borg / WB. Not revisited.
- **Shuttle Ace** — 4W 4/4 with flying on your turn and lifegain when tapped. The Crash Course was split: a "half-lifelink 4/4 flyer" versus "Dirkwood Boars". Not revisited.
- **Shields Up!** — W protection instant (hexproof + indestructible, +1/+1 counter). Mentioned positively in the Crash Course, with no grade.
- **Oberth-Class Starship** — 1W spacecraft that fetches a Plains (2/2 flyer at station 6). It is a Crash Course "shout-out". In 504 it is filler: good in WU, but don't stack it with other low-end spacecraft in a 12–13 creature deck.
- **Pure Energy** — White common sorcery with Beam Me Up that puts counters on things. Named on air as the example of a "travel prep" Beam Me Up card. One host is "not interested" because it fights picking up your own creature (504) (?).
- **Command Decision** — 1W modal: destroy a creature with power 4+, or draw a card. Crash Course: "very good" → 504: **sideboard**. Few things have power 4 (the signposts are x/4s), so it is cycled 80% of the time.
- **Conquest Mission** — 2W enchantment that makes Borg tokens off non-token artifacts. Top-3 white uncommon for both hosts (Crash Course) → 504: "very slow, too small ball, not worth it". The whole Mission cycle is down.
- **Set Phasers to . . .** — W modal instant (stun, or 3 damage to an attacker). Crash Course sleeper → 504: in the sealed deck it never killed anything. "I don't think this is a good card."

### Blue

- **Open Communications** — U draw a card, Beam Me Up 2U. A strong cantrip (Crash Course). In 504 it is excellent: an easy self-timed two-for-one and "bread and butter" for the format. It keeps UB alive.
- **Federation Probe** — U 1/1 flying artifact with a modal ETB (+1/+1 or surveil 1). Called "might be a top common" in the Crash Course (Beam Me Up target + artifact + dilemma). Still "up" in 504.
- **Interstellar Effigy** — 4U 4/3 artifact Man-o'-War. "Obviously good" (Crash Course). "Incredible" in 504.
- **Jett Reno, Problem Solver** — U 1/2 that surveils (or draws at power 4+) when tapped and grows on dilemmas. Crash Course top-3 → 504: "this card is incredible", a continual surveil with spacecraft in WU. It is not a dilemma payoff, "just a good card".
- **Humpback Whales** — 5U 5/6 vigilance with ward 2 (the blue landcycler). In 504 it is "a real player", especially in sealed, because ward 2 makes removal cost a whole turn.
- **Cetacean Ops** — 2U 3/3 with ward 1 that grows on non-creature spells (the second whale). Mentioned in 504, no grade.
- **Make It So** — Blue modal draw-two sorcery (untap / draw two, or a 1/1 officer plus draw two). An "outstanding way to get card advantage" (Crash Course). Not revisited.
- **Guidance Failure** — 3U instant (1 less vs. an attacker) that tops or bottoms a creature or spacecraft. Liked in the Crash Course. 504: "not too shabby", but its hold-up-mana play pattern conflicts with affecting the board.
- **Consider the Prime Directive** — 2U modal: soft counter (unless they pay 3), or stun-tap plus draw. Blue's common counterspell. Same hold-up tension (504).
- **Cloak and Phaser** — 8U sorcery, Strange New Worlds cost reduction. It makes two 2/2 Romulans, then you draw 2 and discard 1. It is the best Strange New Worlds payoff, still good cast for 6–7 off a normal manabase, so blue decks will take it. You can't count on it wheeling (504).
- **V'Ger, the Intruder** — 6U 5/7 flying artifact with a modal ETB / attack trigger. One host's #1 blue uncommon: "an uncommon Titan" (Crash Course). 504: "the one good top end at non-rare".
- **Hoshi Sato, Exolinguist** — 4U 3/3 that digs X by Federation. One host's top-3 (Crash Course). The other: "it's going to feel real bad" at five mana looking at two cards. Not revisited.
- **Zefram Cochrane, Warp Pioneer** — 2U 2/3 that casts artifacts from the top and adds charge counters. One host calls it close to a reason to lean artifacts. The other says it needs 12+ artifacts (Crash Course).
- **Anthropological Mission** — The blue Mission (loots / draws). One host says it "has legs in blue-red", the exception to "all the Missions are bad" (504) (?).
- **Suurok-Class Science Vessel** — 2U spacecraft with an ETB draw (3/2 flyer at station 5). Crash Course: candidate best common → 504: down. It doesn't affect the board, and it needs a 2-drop → Vessel → creature sequence. It is best by far in WU. Don't treat it like a splashable Inspiring Overseer.
- **Energize** — U bounce, Beam Me Up 3U. Ethan's #1 blue uncommon in the Crash Course (the other host was nervous) → 504: less good, because the opponent's board decides when you can beam it up.
- **Marooned** — U aura that freezes an artifact or creature (and blocks Beam Me Up). Liked in the Crash Course → 504 hot take: "I never want to cast this card again". It leaves power on board for power-8, and loses to bounce, flicker and sacrifice.
- **Ensign Pavel Chekov** — 3U 1/5 that exiles opponent lands. "A nothing burger" with an irrelevant body. It is a possible mill-win gimmick with Beam Me Up (504).

### Black

- **Fugitive Listener** — 2B 3/1, ETB each opponent discards. Called a "banger" (Crash Course), possibly better than black's removal and up there with the best commons. 504 confirms that 3 power matters for station, and its 3/1 body makes Khan, Despot by Design better.
- **He's Dead, Jim** — 2B instant: destroy a creature, surveil 1. Excellent removal (both episodes).
- **Eject the Warp Core** — B instant, -2/-2 (kicked by sacrificing an artifact or creature: -6/-6). "A mainstay, very good" in a sacrifice / artifact-token format (Crash Course).
- **Pattern Buffer Preservation** — B sorcery (look at 2, one to hand, one to graveyard), Beam Me Up 1B. "Value all over the place" (Crash Course). 504: excellent, "bread and butter" two-for-one.
- **Resistance Is Futile** — 1B modal: destroy a non-artifact creature, or reanimate an artifact creature with MV ≤3. A top-2 black uncommon for both hosts (Crash Course).
- **In the Pale Moonlight** — 3BB saga: edict, then a clue, then sacrifice for two 2/2s. One host's #1 black uncommon, the other's #3 (Crash Course).
- **D'Ghor Bloodletter** — 2B 2/2 with a modal ETB (all others -1/-1, or each player sacrifices). The floor is high and the ceiling "insane": a one-sided plague wind against 1/1s and 2/1s. It is great to beam up. One host now ranks it over Commander Raffi Musiker (504).
- **Commander Raffi Musiker** — 2B 3/2 with a modal ETB (graveyard hate + 2 life, or investigate − 2 life). It turns off opposing Beam Me Up. A top-3 black uncommon (Crash Course). 504: "high floor, medium ceiling", now slightly behind Bloodletter.
- **Bat'leth** — 2B equipment, ETB -2/-2, equipped +2/+2. One host's #2 black uncommon: "very good" (Crash Course).
- **Organic Avulsion Unit** — 2B 2/3 Borg that grants artifact creatures menace for 2. One of WB's "push it over the top" finishers (504).
- **Automated Warfare System** — 2B 2/2 flying artifact that sacrifices for a card on attack. It "overperformed" for and against one host: evasive, pumpable artifact, and black has fodder (504).
- **Assault Drone** — 3B 2/4 Borg with an artifact-creature +1/+0 anthem. Called "a real player in any artifact deck" (Crash Course). It is WB's common pumper.
- **Relentless Drednok** — B 1/1 lifelink artifact that you can exile from the graveyard to draw. One host is down on it: "didn't do enough" in WB artifacts or as a Beam Me Up one-drop. The other calls it relevant and "gluey" in WB and RB (504).
- **Obsidian Order Operative** — 2B 2/5 that drains on dilemmas. Crash Course: "so good" (one host) → 504: "not a thing in this format… below filler".
- **Tell Me What You See** — 1B dilemma build-around enchantment. Played twice in one deck: "pretty bad". The opponent just takes the life loss unless you are already pressuring them (504).
- **Collation Trimatrix** — B 1/1 Borg rare (tap three artifact creatures: draw, lose life). This was the first-pick build-around that one host regrets drafting around. Example of "don't get cute" (504).
- **Battle-Scarred Survivalist** — 1B 1/1 menace that grows when a creature dies. Crash Course sleeper → 504: down. It is a 2-mana 1/1 that stations poorly and you never want to beam up.
- **Vengeance Mission** — The black Mission (cares about creatures dying). It is the only Mission one host still theorycrafts, for RB Klingon-token sacrifice loops. Otherwise "the Missions are not good" (504) (?).

### Red

- **Khan, Despot by Design** — 4R 5/4 trample, ETB 1 damage to each opponent and each of their creatures. "Top uncommon vibes", since there are so many X/1s. It often plague-winds, and it is great hasted with The Naked Time (504).
- **Planetary Bombardment** — R sorcery, 2 damage (4 if a creature died this turn). Crash Course: "not great" (sorcery speed) → 504: "just good", not clunky.
- **Plasma Cascade** — 3-mana 3 to any target plus a loot. Crash Course: barely usable → 504: "just good".
- **Boarding Action** — 1R sorcery: a 2/1 haste Klingon, Beam Me Up 2R. It was one host's only red notable common ("if red works, this is why"). 504 confirms a top common: 2/1s are a good stat line, and it gives a self-timed Beam Me Up.
- **Munitions Enthusiast** — 1R 3/2 trample that pings on death. "A banger rate card" (Crash Course).
- **Transporter Malfunction** — 1R sorcery, 3 damage, Beam Me Up (its cost is garbled in the transcript (?)). Both hosts' #1 red uncommon (Crash Course).
- **Khaaaaaaaaaaaannn!** — RX instant, 2X damage to a creature, exile. Both hosts' #2 red uncommon, scaling well (Crash Course).
- **La'An Noonien-Singh, Security** — 4-mana 4/3. When you attack with power 8+, a creature can't block. It shows how easy power-8 is: "half your threshold" (504).
- **The Naked Time** — 1R saga (fetch a Mountain tapped, then creatures entering get +1/+1 and haste, then sacrifice a land). One host: "unplayable → very playable", explosive power-8 turns. The other is unsure it is worth a card (504).
- **Dahar Master** — 3R 4/3, ETB may sacrifice another nonland permanent to deal 2 to any target. It "has legs". Sacrifice sagas before chapter III (504).
- **Silicate Surveyor** — 4R 4/4 reach with an impulse-draw ETB. Crash Course split (good splash vs. "too slow") → 504: "impressed", "a brick wall" two-for-one with no setup.
- **Cantankerous Captain** — 2R 2/3 combat pump + menace at power 8+. One host liked it. The other: "a nothing burger" (Crash Course). Its 504 note is that it helps reach power-8.
- **Perils of the Past** — 1R modal instant (+3/+2, or destroy an artifact). One host says it's main-deckable *for the artifact mode* in this artifact-heavy format. The other doubts it makes the non-creature cut (504).
- **Targ Wrangler** — 2R 5/3 menace that sacrifices a nonland permanent if it attacked. Debated: five-eighths of power-8 and a saga sacrifice outlet, versus a real cost against "don't get cute" (504). Not seen in play.
- **Academy Instructor** — 3-mana officer that grows and makes tokens off non-creature spells. One host's #3 red uncommon. The other dislikes "creatures that are non-creature payoffs" (Crash Course).
- **Planetary Patrol** — 1R 2/2 landfall pump. Be cautious about it: it is embarrassing on turns 6–8 (504).

### Multicolor

- **Constable Odo** — 3 + {W/U}{W/U} 4/3, ETB tap and double-stun, or investigate twice. "Might be the mythic uncommon of the set… cracked." It is one of the best Beam Me Up targets, and one of the few uncommons worth stretching for (not quite a double splash) (504).
- **Vash, Freelance Archaeologist** — BG 2/4 uncommon, upkeep surveil 1, draws when a card leaves your graveyard. "Pushed" (Crash Course). It is the BG anchor.
- **Locutus, the One Who Speaks** — WB 1/1 legendary Borg lord (artifact creatures +1/+1) that you can discard for 2 damage and 2 life. A good early take to test WB (Crash Course).
- **Seven of Nine** — 2UB 2/4 that draws when you face a dilemma. It was the UB signpost in the Crash Course list. The 504 verdict on dilemma ("not a thing") applies to it.
- **Kirk, Commanding Officer** — RW 3/3. When you attack with power 8+, impulse the top card. Pay 2 and sacrifice to destroy an artifact or enchantment (e.g. Malfunctioning Holodeck). "Keeps the value flowing" in RW (504).

### Colorless

- **Warp Core** — 2-mana artifact that fetches a basic land and can add charge counters to a spacecraft. "A sixth landcycler". Better in Esper because it is an artifact entering (Crash Course).
- **Class-F Shuttlecraft** — 3-mana mana rock spacecraft (2/4 flying vigilance at station 6). One host likes it more than Warp Core, the other less. Play it only in the soupiest decks (Crash Course).
